Extending NAMER for Learning-Enhanced MRI Motion Estimation

NAMER uses a CNN-generated image prior to estimate motion before model-based reconstruction. Current work is expanding evaluation to multiple fastMRI subjects and revising CNN training so the prior better supports downstream motion estimation.
